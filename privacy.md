# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 3, 2026_

## Word42, the application

Word42 is a free word processor, licensed under the GPL, that runs
entirely on your own computer.

- Word42 collects no personal data.
- Word42 does not send your documents, your text or anything else over
  the network. It has no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting
  and no update checks.
- Your documents stay on your computer, in files you control.

## word42.org, the website

This website is a set of static pages with no cookies, no analytics and
no tracking. It is hosted on
[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), and GitHub may record basic
technical information about visits (such as IP addresses) in its server
logs, as described in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
We do not receive or store any of that information ourselves.

## Contact
